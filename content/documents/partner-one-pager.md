## What it does for your work

- **Fractional COO or CFO: your engagement keeps moving.** When the fix is clear and the founder won't let go of the decisions it depends on, bring me in. You keep the client, and you're in the room when we change who decides what.
- **Recruiter: your placement sticks.** Offer your client a free 45-minute Handoff Kickoff with the founder and the new hire in the first weeks. They leave with last week's decisions sorted into the founder's, the new hire's and unclear.
- **Startup lawyer: your client may not need the exit.** When a founder asks how to let a senior hire go, send them to me first. If it turns out to be the setup, the hire can stay.
- **You run a room: your founders get a live clinic.** A free 60 minutes on "Why does everything still depend on the founder?" Two of them bring a real decision. Nobody gets pitched.

**No fees in either direction, and your client stays yours.**

## How to spot it

- The founder takes a week off and things slow down.
- The founder says "I hired a COO and I'm still the COO."
- The founder is starting to wonder whether they hired the wrong person. That's the best moment to bring me in: **before anyone gets fired.**

## What your client gets

The founder hired a COO or a Head of Ops so things would stop going through them. It all still goes through them. **The Handoff** moves those decisions onto the person they hired in 30 days. We count what reaches the founder before and after. If the count hasn't dropped by 30%, the second month is on me.

It starts with a 90-minute diagnostic for €750, and the founder gets a written diagnosis. If The Handoff is worth doing, they book it within 30 days of the session and the €750 comes off its price.

## After you introduce someone

You get a short note within two days of the first session: whether it went ahead, and what the founder is OK with me sharing. Everything else stays with the founder.

If they'd rather reach out themselves, send them this:

> "You hired good people and decisions still come back to you. Trung works out whether it's the person or the setup: iamtrung.com/d/diagnostic-handout"
