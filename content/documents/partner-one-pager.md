## What I do

A founder hired a COO or a Head of Ops so things would stop going through them. It all still goes through them. I run **the Handoff**: 30 days to move those decisions onto the person they hired. We count what reaches the founder before and after. If the count hasn't dropped by 30%, the second month is on me.

**No fees in either direction, and your client stays yours.** I don't do the implementation and I don't take over the relationship.

[Tell me who](https://tally.so/r/1WWMJ4). You don't need to name them yet. Describe the situation and I'll tell you within one business day whether it fits.

## What it does for your work

- **Fractional COO or CFO.** Your fix is clear and the founder won't let go of the decisions it depends on. Bring me in and keep the engagement. You're in the room when we change who decides what.
- **Recruiter.** Offer your client a free 45-minute Handoff Kickoff with the founder and the new hire, in the first weeks. The placement has a better chance of sticking.
- **You run a room.** I run a free 60-minute live clinic on "Why does everything still depend on the founder?" Two founders bring a real decision. Nobody gets pitched.

## When to think of me

- Decisions the new hire was supposed to own still land on the founder's desk.
- The founder takes a week off and things slow down.
- The founder says "I hired a COO and I'm still the COO."
- The founder is starting to wonder whether they hired the wrong person. That's the best moment to call me: **before anyone gets fired.**

## After you introduce someone

Within two days of the first session you get a short note: whether it went ahead, and what the founder is OK with me sharing. Everything else stays with the founder. If it's not a fit, I say so directly.

If they'd rather reach out themselves, send them this:

> "You hired good people and decisions still come back to you. My friend Trung works out why, and whether the fix is the person or the setup: linkedin.com/in/ctn1991. DM him 'diagnostic' to start."
