## What I do

A founder hired a COO or a Head of Ops so things would stop going through them. It all still goes through them. I run **The Handoff**: 30 days to move those decisions onto the person they hired. We count what reaches the founder before and after. If the count hasn't dropped by 30%, the second month is on me. It starts with a 90-minute diagnostic for €750. If The Handoff is worth doing, they book it within 30 days of the session and the €750 comes off its price.

**No fees in either direction, and your client stays yours.**

## What it does for your work

- **Fractional COO or CFO.** Your fix is clear and the founder won't let go of the decisions it depends on. Bring me in and keep the engagement. You're in the room when we change who decides what.
- **Recruiter.** Offer your client a free 45-minute Handoff Kickoff with the founder and the new hire in the first weeks. They leave with last week's decisions sorted into the founder's, the new hire's and unclear.
- **Startup lawyer.** When a founder asks how to let a senior hire go, send them to me first. If it turns out to be the setup, your client may not need the exit at all.
- **You run a room.** I run a free 60-minute live clinic on "Why does everything still depend on the founder?" Two founders bring a real decision. Nobody gets pitched.

## When to think of me

- The founder takes a week off and things slow down.
- The founder says "I hired a COO and I'm still the COO."
- The founder is starting to wonder whether they hired the wrong person. That's the best moment to call me: **before anyone gets fired.**
