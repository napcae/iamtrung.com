You should be able to tell your COO what you got in two sentences. If you can't, the session missed.

Before we meet: until the session, write down every decision that reaches you, up to five working days. One line each: what it was and who sent it. In the session we sort them into three piles: yours, someone else's, and the ones nobody ever decided. If you've hired leaders, the last two piles are where the hire-or-setup answer starts.

Who this is for

A founder-led company with customers and a team, where you've hired a COO or Head of Ops, or are hiring one, to take decisions off you. If you don't have a team yet, this isn't the right first step.

The pattern

Most of the money founders have paid me came from this situation: the company still ran through them, whatever they'd already tried. Bringing in managers solves one growing pain and starts the next one, because the authority doesn't move with the title.

The cost

If it's the setup and you replace the person, you pay for a search and wait months with the seat empty. Then the next hire lands in the same setup. If you're about to replace your leader or send them on a leadership program, check the setup first: both bet on the person. You can postpone the decision. You can't postpone what the current system keeps costing you.
